Button Control Guide

Control your DPV using the left and right buttons with these commands:

Left Button Right Button Function
Hold Hold Turn motor ON
Hold - Turn motor ON
- Hold Turn motor ON
1 Click 1 Click Cruise Control (within 150ms)
2 Clicks - Speed Down
- 2 Clicks Speed Up
2 Clicks 2 Clicks Wake up from Standby
2 Clicks + Hold 2 Clicks + Hold Turbo Mode (Maximum Power)
3 Clicks - Short Light Flash
- 3 Clicks Toggle Light Level (0→1→2→3→4→0)
3 Clicks 3 Clicks PowerBank ON/OFF (Not implemented)
4 Clicks - Battery Level Beep Output

Beep Codes Reference

Understanding system status through audio signals:

Legend: 1 = short beep, 2 = long beep

Beep Pattern Meaning
12121212 ⚠️ LEAK WARNING
1 Still in standby / Boot confirmation
11 Going to standby / Wake up from standby
111 No speed up (overloaded)
111222111 SOS Signal (Long standby)
2 10% battery remaining
22 20% battery remaining
222 30% battery remaining
n×2 Battery level in 10% steps (4 clicks command)
